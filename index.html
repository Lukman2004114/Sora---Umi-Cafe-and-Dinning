Our Philosophy · 哲学

Between Heaven
and Ocean

Sora & Umi is built upon the philosophy of wabi-sabi — the beauty found in impermanence, simplicity, and the natural world. We believe that a meal is not merely sustenance; it is a passage through time, light, and water.

From the pale blush of sakura drifting through a morning sky, to the stillness of deep ocean where bioluminescent creatures drift in silence — every moment at our table is a quiet invitation to be present.

Our Aquarium · 水族館

The Floating World

Discover our underwater residents — each one a living artwork.

Koi Fish 錦鯉

Living jewels drifting through liquid light — embodying perseverance, strength, and good fortune in Japanese culture.

Moon Jellyfish 月水母

Ethereal drifters pulsing in silence — they move with a rhythm as ancient as the tides, illuminated by their own quiet light.

Sea Turtle 海亀

Ancient navigators of the deep, they glide with unhurried grace — living symbols of longevity, wisdom, and the ocean's enduring patience.

Bluefin Tuna 本鮪

The sovereign of open water — sleek, powerful, and breathtaking in motion. A creature that has commanded reverence in Japanese culture for centuries.

The Experience · 体験

Dine Beneath
the Ocean

At Sora & Umi, your table is surrounded not by walls, but by water. Bioluminescent creatures drift past floor-to-ceiling glass as you savour each course. The aquarium is not a backdrop — it is a living participant in your meal.

Every evening, our chefs narrate a journey from the sky to the abyss through seven courses — each dish mirroring a depth zone of the ocean, from the sunlit shallows to the midnight trenches where light itself becomes a rarity.
