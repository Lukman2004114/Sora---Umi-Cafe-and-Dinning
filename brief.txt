==============================================================
SITE: Sora & Umi Café & Dining
==============================

## ROLE / CONTEXT

Role: You are a Creative Director designing a premium immersive web experience.

Context:
Sora & Umi Café & Dining is a Japanese-themed underwater fish museum combined with a café and restaurant. The experience is designed as a visual journey from sky (Sora) to sea (Umi), blending cultural storytelling with modern web design.

The goal is to create a website that feels calm, elegant, immersive, and interactive — similar to a real-world premium brand.

---

## BUILD STACK

1. Stitch (layout generation)
2. UI/UX Tool (style, spacing, typography)
3. Firecrawl (reference research)
4. Nanobanana (visual assets and mood)
5. Claude (final development: HTML, CSS, JavaScript)

---

## PURPOSE

A digital experience representing a Japanese-inspired café and restaurant set inside an underwater museum. The site allows users to explore marine exhibits, view menus, and experience a relaxing immersive environment.

==============================================================
STEP 2 — BRAND VOICE
====================

Voice archetype:
Calm Storyteller — minimal, elegant, peaceful, slightly poetic

Tone:

* Formal ↔ Casual:        3 (balanced)
* Serious ↔ Playful:      2 (calm, not childish)
* Reserved ↔ Expressive:  3 (soft expression)
* Modern ↔ Timeless:      4 (timeless aesthetic)

Voice rules:

* Use short, clear sentences
* Focus on calm and immersive language
* Avoid slang or overly playful tone
* Speak directly to the user

Key phrases:

* “Where Sky Meets Sea”
* “A journey beneath the waves”
* “Experience calm, depth, and flavor”
* “From clouds to ocean depths”

==============================================================
STEP 3 — DESIGN SYSTEM
======================

Style keywords:
minimal, Japanese, calm, immersive, modern, clean, elegant

COLOR SYSTEM:

* Primary:        Sky blue
* Secondary:      Deep ocean blue
* Accent:         Sakura pink
* Neutral:        Soft beige / off-white
* Text:           Dark blue / soft black

TYPOGRAPHY:

* Heading: modern Japanese-inspired serif
* Body: clean sans-serif (high readability)
* Scale: consistent hierarchy (H1 → H6)
* Line height: 1.5–1.6

SPACING:

* Consistent vertical rhythm
* Clean section separation
* Responsive spacing

LAYOUT:

* Grid-based (Flexbox / CSS Grid)
* Mobile-first design
* Balanced whitespace

MOTION:

* Smooth scrolling
* Fade + slide animations
* Subtle floating effects (fish, particles)
* Soft transitions (no aggressive motion)
